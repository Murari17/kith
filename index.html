Encrypted chat that works without internet.

Pair face to face with a QR code and chat over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct, even in airplane mode. When you’re online, messages also sync through decentralized Nostr relays. No phone number, no account, no server.

Android 7 or newer. No servers.

Private by design, not by policy.

Works in airplane mode

Bluetooth LE and Wi-Fi Direct carry messages across multiple hops, so nearby phones relay encrypted packets for each other. No signal needed.

Nostr fallback when online

Messages are encrypted with NIP-44 and signed with BIP-340 Schnorr, then synced through public Nostr relays. They arrive even if your contact is offline, and duplicates are dropped automatically.

Burner rooms and panic wipe

Start a five-minute group room from a QR code, send compressed photos up to 18 KB with end-to-end encryption, set auto-delete timers, or erase the database and keys with one tap.
